About Me

Curious, reflective, and steadily building a life I’m proud of.

When entering college in the initial semesters, most students feel confused about what direction to take, what skills to learn, and how to start. I was in the exact same boat. But I realized that taking consistent small steps forward is far better than standing still in overthinking or regret.

My foundation and biggest pillars of motivation are my parents, who have supported my journey every step of the way. They are truly the bedrock of my "house of life".

I have always been fascinated by the vast ocean of technology and its endless possibilities. Choosing Computer Science was my personal choice and passion. I am currently dedicated to exploring modern programming languages and building software that creates tangible real-world value.
